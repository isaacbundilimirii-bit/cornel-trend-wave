Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Next Wave Trends.

How do I place an order?
Browse our products, click "Add to cart", then go to your cart and click "Checkout". Choose delivery or pickup, enter your details, select payment method (M-Pesa, card, or cash on delivery), and place your order. You'll get a WhatsApp confirmation.
How long does delivery take?
Nairobi (CBD & environs): Same day. Nairobi outer: Same/Next day. Major towns (Mombasa, Kisumu): 1-2 days. Other areas: 2-3 days. Free delivery on orders over Ksh 3,000.
Can I pick up my order?
Yes! Choose "Pickup" at checkout and collect your order at our Nairobi shop. No delivery fee. We'll WhatsApp you when it's ready.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept M-Pesa (Lipa na M-Pesa till), card payments, and cash on delivery. M-Pesa is the fastest — you'll see our till number at checkout.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 7-day return policy. Items must be unused and in original packaging. WhatsApp us at +254 759 051 333 to arrange a return or exchange. See our Returns Policy for details.
Are your products genuine?
Yes! All our electronics are genuine and come with warranty where applicable. Fashion items are brand new and quality-checked before dispatch.
How do I track my order?
Once dispatched, you'll receive a WhatsApp message with tracking details. You can also check your order status in your account under "Orders".
Do I need an account to order?
You can order without an account, but creating one lets you track orders, save your address for faster checkout, earn loyalty points, and get exclusive offers. It's free — sign up here.
Can I order via WhatsApp?
Absolutely! Click the green WhatsApp button on any product to order directly. Our team will confirm availability and arrange delivery.
How do I contact customer support?
We're available 24/7 via WhatsApp (+254 759 051 333), phone call, or email (hello@nextwavetrends.co.ke). Visit our Contact page for all options.
